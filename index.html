Introduction

The definition of technological development and the spread of digital services, and the emergence of chatbots as a means to facilitate communication between users and electronic systems.

Chatbots are computer programs designed to communicate with people through conversation, whether by text, voice, or answering their questions and helping them accomplish certain tasks.

Body Content

How do chatbots work?

1. The user asks a question, for example: What are the working hours?

2. The program processes the question and tries to determine what is required either using predefined rules or artificial intelligence techniques.

3. The program responds to the user with the registered information.

Types of Chatbots

Robot Type How it Works & Examples
Traditional Chatbots Rely on predefined rules, questions, and answers such as choosing "shipping" or "returns" from a list.
AI-Powered Chatbots Use techniques to understand questions in different formats and generate appropriate responses like ChatGPT

Importance and Challenges: Saving time, fast response, helping companies with customer service, and assisting students, but facing challenges such as potential errors in answers and privacy issues.
